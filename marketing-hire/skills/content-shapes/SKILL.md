# Shapes

Use one shape. Do not mix them into a tour of other people's careers.

- Repeat one thesis until it is boring. Change the angle, not the words.
- One primary channel. A long piece can become a few posts. The posts are not the essay pasted shorter.
- A hook carries a number, a date, or a decision that is already in the notes. If it is not, do not borrow one.
- Show the work. A lesson hangs on a specific choice the company made.
- Coin a few phrases and reuse them. Do not coin a new vocabulary every week.
- The customer's words, from a note or a call, become the hook.

Do not copy another account's frequency, a performed expert tone, or a take about a field the company does not work in. Do not paste their sentences.

A posting tool waits until the primary channel is a habit.
