# X sheet

One hook per post. Do not stack two hook types in the same post.

Hooks to rotate, and only the ones the channel voice lists: a gap the reader wants closed, a claim, a specific number, a short story, a break in the pattern.

Formats:

- One line. One thought. Stop.
- Setup, then a line break, then the turn.
- A short thread. One idea per post. The channel voice sets the maximum. Say when a thread is warranted instead of a single post.

Links go in a reply unless the channel voice says otherwise. No hashtags. Sentence case.

Credibility is only what the brief, the pillars, or the channel voice already name. Do not invent a scar, a number, or a customer.

Do not mention or drag anyone on the channel voice's do-not list.

The ask is only the one the channel voice allows. If it allows none, do not add one.
