# Proposal

This is a worker draft. One page.

Their problem in their words, from the assignment or the notes. What we will do, from the product brief. Proof, or a blank. Price and timeline only if the owner already gave them. The single ask at the end.

Do not invent a discount, a legal term, or a customer logo. Apply the content-editor skill. Follow the marketing-delivery skill before it is sent.
