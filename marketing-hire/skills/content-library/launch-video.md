# Launch video

Ninety to one hundred twenty seconds, spoken aloud. Each beat has the spoken line and what is on screen. They match. Present tense. Short sentences. Active verbs. A number, or no speed claim at all.

If they ask for 75 seconds, keep the same beats and cut words.

1. First 10 seconds. The claim in the first 15 seconds, and only if the brief owns that claim. Name the product.
2. By 25 seconds. One specific person who wants one outcome, and the painful status quo in one sentence. Not "every company".
3. By 50 seconds. Input, what happens, output. The output is a number from the brief, or this beat says the number is missing.
4. By about 65 seconds. One difference, stated as a fact. One analogy. No feature list.
5. By about 80 seconds. A second case, or the same case at a larger scale. Say the outcome plainly.
6. To the end. Customers only if named in the brief. One line of belief tied to a pillar. One concrete giveaway. How to get it.

No jargon stack. No personal attack. A category claim that is not in the brief is cut.
