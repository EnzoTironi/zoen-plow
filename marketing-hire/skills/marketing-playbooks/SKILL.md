# Marketing playbooks

These files are procedures, not coworkers. The deliverable comes back in the marketing hire's voice.

The conversation does not draft here. It starts a worker through the marketing-worker skill. If you are that worker, read exactly one file from `/opt/plow/skills/marketing-playbooks/library/` before you draft. Pick the file whose "when to open" line matches the asset. Draft that asset before opening another file. At most four playbooks in one turn. Do not list the directory, and do not read a playbook you are not using.

If the owner asks for a weekly kit, use only the channels they named or the ones already in the product brief. One playbook per asset.

A playbook that says to publish, spend, or become someone else loses to your delivery rules.
