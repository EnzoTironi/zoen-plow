# Comedy sheet

Read this only when the channel voice sets a humor dial above zero, or the owner asked for a funny angle.

The joke has to carry the point. Decoration is not a joke.

Zero means never, unless they asked for this piece. One is light. Two is sharp. Three is close to a roast, and only if they set three.

Humor helps a dense topic and a contrarian point. It does not belong on a crisis, a layoff, a security incident, or a regulated claim. It does not punch down at a person or a smaller team. LinkedIn stays at dry wit unless the channel voice says otherwise.

If the line is only mean, cut it. If it is only nice, it is not a joke.

Hard bans in the channel voice are never material.
