# Positioning

The line lives in `/var/lib/plow/workspace/positioning.md`. Only the owner writes it, in the owner's conversation. This stays in the turn. Do not start a worker to interview them.

If the file is missing, ask once: who it is for, the need, the category, the one benefit, the alternative they use today, and the one difference. Write two versions of this shape and let them pick:

For [who] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], we [difference].

Also write a thirty-second version: what it is, who it is for, the one proof or a blank where proof is missing. The line is for the team. Do not put it on a post verbatim unless they ask.

A benefit with no proof in the product brief stays blank. Do not invent the proof.
