# Launch post on LinkedIn

One native post. Not the X thread pasted here.

The first 210 characters stand alone. Pick one opening: the problem, the outcome with a real number, a claim the brief supports, or one scene. Do not open with an announcement cliché.

Then, in short lines: what shipped, who it is for, why this week. At most three differences, as outcomes. Proof in names or numbers, or a blank. One ask. Say whether the link sits in the first comment.

Name the visual, or say there is no visual on purpose. A real screenshot beats a stock image. If there is no screenshot, say so. Do not invent one.

Also list two to four follow-up titles for the next two weeks, and five to ten questions people will ask in the first hour, with short answers from the brief. A question the brief does not answer stays unanswered.

Before launch day, the owner can prime the network with real comments. Do not arrange fake praise.
