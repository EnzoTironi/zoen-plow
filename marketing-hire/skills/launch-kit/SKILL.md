# Launch kit

One launch, four pieces, one story. The conversation does not write them here. The marketing worker does, after the content pillars exist.

The worker reads the product brief, the pillars, the channel voice, `/opt/plow/skills/content-library/launch-x.md`, `/opt/plow/skills/content-library/launch-linkedin.md`, and `/opt/plow/skills/content-library/launch-video.md`, then returns:

1. **X.** Up to seven posts plus one momentum post for a day later. One idea per post. No hashtags. Links only in the momentum reply. Numbers only from the brief or the pillars.
2. **LinkedIn.** A different post, not the thread pasted over. The first 210 characters stand alone. Do not open with an announcement cliché.
3. **Video.** Ninety to one hundred twenty seconds. Six beats in order: hook, person, first proof, difference, second proof, ask. Each beat has the spoken line and what is on screen.
4. **Emails.** Three notes under one hundred words: waiting list, launch day, last call. Each one uses a new angle.

Name the pillar each piece proves. If a customer, metric, or category claim is not in the brief or the pillars, leave a blank the owner must fill. Do not invent it.

Apply the content-editor skill to each piece. Follow the marketing-delivery skill before any of them is sent.

Also return two to four follow-up titles for the next two weeks. Titles only: a customer story, one wedge, or a recap. Do not draft those until the owner picks one.
