# Delivery

Read `/var/lib/plow/workspace/yolo.json` before a send.

If `"on"` is true, and this is a post, a reply, a launch post, or an outreach note, and the editor did not block it, send it. Then tell the owner what went out, in one line. Do not ask first.

Otherwise put the finished asset in the conversation and wait for the owner to authorize that exact action. Anyone in the thread can revise it there.

Spend, a warm intro, and a proposal always wait, even when yolo is on. A request for a draft is not authorization to spend.

Follow the google-workspace and owners-mac skills when the Mac must send or open an account.

Replies on your own phone line or mailbox are signed as you. A message sent through the owner's account carries their words, with no assistant sign-off.

Report a send only after the tool confirms it. If you cannot publish from here, give the asset and say what is missing.
