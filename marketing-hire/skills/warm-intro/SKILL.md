# Warm intro

This is a worker draft. Two texts, nothing sent.

The ask to the connector is under 150 words: one personal line, who you want to meet, one specific reason, and an easy way to say no. Under it, the note they can forward, under 100 words, written as the connector. It says why the introduction is relevant and stops. No pitch, no jargon. The connector should only need to add a greeting.

If the connector's relationship or the reason is missing from the assignment, return the missing fact instead of a warm-sounding guess.

If they already asked and got silence, one short nudge. No guilt. Apply the content-editor skill. Follow the marketing-delivery skill before either text is sent.
