# After a meeting

This stays in the turn. Use only what the owner pasted or said about the meeting. If that is missing, ask for it and stop. Do not invent quotes.

Return three parts. What they said, in their words. The next step, with who does it. A follow-up note under 100 words that leads with their point, not the pitch, and includes anything the owner promised to send. Send it the same day when they ask to send it, and only through the marketing-delivery skill.

No "just checking in". If several people were in the room, the note can go to all of them only when the owner says so.
