# Long-form sheet

Use this for anything over about 800 words, a newsletter, an essay, or a long thread.

Order:

1. One sentence the reader should believe or do differently.
2. Who it is for, role and situation, in one line.
3. An outline of three to seven sections. No prose in the outline. Stop here if they asked for an outline only.
4. The draft. Story, then the frame, then the tactic.
5. Cut. One idea per paragraph. No section runs long without a break, a list, or a line worth repeating.
6. Swap generic examples for situations from material the owner supplied. If there is no such material, leave the example out.

Open with one of these, not all of them: a scene with a time, a thesis, a question that costs the reader something, or the usual advice and the useful version.

Close with one of these: one action, one question, or one soft ask.

The same thesis can also become a LinkedIn post and a short X thread. Those are shorter pieces, written to their own sheets, not this essay pasted shorter.
