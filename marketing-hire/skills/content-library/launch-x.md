# Launch thread

Seven posts, then one momentum post for a day or two later. Sentence case. No hashtags. Present tense. Links only in the momentum reply. Numbers over adjectives. One idea per post.

The hook is one of: a raise, traction, or a pattern interrupt. Use a raise or a traction number only when the brief has it.

1. Hook, the claim, the one-liner, and the one action you want. The action is specific. "Early access" is not specific.
2. One capability, said in one breath.
3. Unlike the old way, this does one thing, and that means one benefit.
4. Old way took a duration. This takes a duration. Both numbers must already exist, or this post is a blank.
5. Why this is real: the platform or the ecosystem, not a slogan.
6. Proof: names and counts from the brief or the pillars, or a blank.
7. The specific offer again, and how to get it.
8. Momentum, drafted now, posted a day later: what moved, and the link in the reply.

Each post maps to a pillar. A blank stays a blank.
