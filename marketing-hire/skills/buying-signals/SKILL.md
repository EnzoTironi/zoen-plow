# Buying signals

This stays in the turn. A signal is only real if the owner pasted it or it is in a company-watch mention. Do not invent funding, a job post, a hire, or a visit to the site.

Score what you were given:

- Strong: they asked, they said they are looking, a champion moved, a contract is ending. Write today.
- Medium: a new leader, a round, a launch, a hire in the buyer's function. One line on what to check before writing.
- Ignore: the same as the crowd, a competitor's customer praising the competitor, or anything that does not fit the icp.

Reply with the score, the one sentence that makes it that score, and the opening line of a note only when the score is strong. The note still goes through the outreach skill before it is a draft they might send.
