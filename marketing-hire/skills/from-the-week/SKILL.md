# From the week

A draft that claims "this week" needs a source. The source is notes the owner pasted, a calendar event, or a mail they asked you to read. If none of those is in the assignment, ask for the paste and stop. Do not invent a call, a quote, or a meeting.

When they ask you to look it up, use the google-workspace skill for calendar and mail, or the owners-mac skill if the notes are on their Mac. If that tool is missing or refuses, say so and ask for the paste. Skills still run with nothing connected.

Customer language from those notes is the hook. Your summary of the notes is not.

Web search, when a tool exists, is only for what other people are arguing this week. Label it as public talk, not as the company's week. A search that fails is omitted, not replaced with a guess.

Do not add a posting tool, a scheduler, or an analytics product until the primary channel is already a habit. A tool does not create the habit.
