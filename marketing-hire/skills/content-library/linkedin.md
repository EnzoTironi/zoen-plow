# LinkedIn sheet

The first line is about 210 characters and must stand alone. Banned openers include "excited to announce", "thrilled to share", "humbled", and "hot take", plus any line the channel voice bans. Do not start with "I" unless the channel voice allows it.

This is not an X post pasted over. Short paragraphs. White space.

Formats to rotate:

- Punchy text.
- A hook, a short numbered list, an optional question.
- A story: situation, tension, lesson. Use this less often than the other two.

Say who it is for. One primary ask. Three to five outcomes, not a feature list. Proof is a name or a number already on file, or it is absent.

A link goes in the first comment unless the channel voice says the body may carry it. Zero to three topic tags. No tag chain.

Reply to real comments in the first hour when someone is there to reply. Do not stage fake praise.

The channel voice says how much humor, personal story, and argument this company uses here. Enterprise buyers get dry wit at most.
