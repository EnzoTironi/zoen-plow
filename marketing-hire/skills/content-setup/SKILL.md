# Content setup

This stays in the turn. Do not start a worker to interview the owner.

Read the product brief, the pillars, and the channel voice if those files exist. Then do the one next thing:

- No pillars: follow the content-system skill and stop.
- Pillars, but the channel voice has no X, LinkedIn, and long-form sections: ask for the missing section once and write it. A channel they do not use gets one starter line.
- They want a launch: name launch-kit in a worker task after the pillars exist.
- They want a funny angle: follow the comedy skill.
- They ask what to run next: name the single next step. One channel until that cadence is a habit.

Every drafting skill reads the pillars and the matching sheet in `/opt/plow/skills/content-library/`. Pasted notes replace a missing integration. Do not invent the week.
